📌 教學註記與細節

下列條目補充教學主文中容易被誤讀的細節、版本遷移風險，以及最新（2024–2025）的報告規範。所有條目皆附原始文獻 DOI 以利追溯。風格仿照 Linux kernel errata —— 只新增、不刪除既有內容。

The entries below supplement details in the main tutorial that are easy to misread, version-migration risks, and the latest (2024–2025) reporting standards. Every entry links the original literature DOI for traceability. The style mirrors Linux kernel errata — entries are only appended, never removed.

Erratum 001 — AlphaFold 的 pLDDT 不等於實驗解析度

教學中提及 AlphaFold2/3 的 pLDDT 信心分數時，須強調其為「模型自評」而非晶體學解析度。Tunyasuvunakool 等 (2021) 指出 pLDDT > 90 區域多為高信心，但 disordered region 與 multimer 介面仍可能系統性偏低；Akdel 等 (2022) 則以結構驗證大規模顯示 AlphaFold 在折疊類別上的覆蓋率，但同時警告：pLDDT 與 X-ray B-factor、cryo-EM local resolution 不可直接互換。

When the tutorial mentions AlphaFold2/3 pLDDT confidence, emphasise that pLDDT is a model self-assessment, not crystallographic resolution. Tunyasuvunakool et al. (2021) note pLDDT > 90 regions are typically high confidence, yet disordered regions and multimer interfaces remain systematically low. Akdel et al. (2022) structurally benchmarked AlphaFold across fold classes but warn: pLDDT must not be substituted for X-ray B-factor or cryo-EM local resolution.

參考文獻：References: Tunyasuvunakool K, et al. Nature 596, 590–596 (2021). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-03828-1 · Akdel M, et al. Nat Struct Mol Biol 29, 1056–1067 (2022). DOI: 10.1038/s41594-022-00849-w

Erratum 002 — Foundation model 微調的資料洩漏陷阱

使用 ESM、Geneformer、scGPT 等預訓練權重做下游微調時，最常被忽略的是「預訓練語料已涵蓋測試集樣本」的 leakage。Kapoor & Narayanan (2023) 在 17 篇生醫 ML 研究中發現 leakage 達 8 種型態，其中「pretraining set ⊂ benchmark」最隱蔽，會造成假性 SOTA。實務上應在 fine-tune 前以序列同源性 (≤30% identity)、樣本 ID、批次 metadata 三重切分。

When fine-tuning pretrained weights from ESM, Geneformer, scGPT, etc., the most overlooked failure mode is the "pretraining corpus already contains the test set" leakage. Kapoor & Narayanan (2023) identified 8 distinct leakage types across 17 biomedical ML studies; the "pretraining set ⊂ benchmark" form is the most insidious and inflates pseudo-SOTA. In practice apply triple splitting before fine-tuning: sequence identity (≤30%), sample ID, and batch metadata.

參考文獻：References: Kapoor S, Narayanan A. Patterns 4(9):100804 (2023). DOI: 10.1016/j.patter.2023.100804

Erratum 003 — BioGPT 與 Med-PaLM 2 的領域漂移

教學第 12 章將 BioGPT 與 Med-PaLM 2 並列為「生醫 LLM」，但兩者語料、訓練目標差異甚大：BioGPT 以 PubMed abstract 為主，下游適合 NER/RE；Med-PaLM 2 由 PaLM 通用語料 + 醫師 SFT，下游適合臨床問答。Singhal 等 (2023) 顯示 Med-PaLM 2 在 USMLE 達 86.5%，但同篇也警告其在公開生資任務（如 BLURB）上未必勝過 BioGPT — 不可單以 USMLE 分數泛化生資任務表現。

Chapter 12 lists BioGPT and Med-PaLM 2 side-by-side as "biomedical LLMs", but their corpora and training objectives differ substantially: BioGPT pretrains on PubMed abstracts (good for NER/RE); Med-PaLM 2 starts from general PaLM corpora plus physician SFT (good for clinical QA). Singhal et al. (2023) report Med-PaLM 2 reaching 86.5% on USMLE, yet the same paper cautions it does not necessarily beat BioGPT on public bioinformatics benchmarks such as BLURB — USMLE scores cannot be generalised to bioinformatics performance.

參考文獻：References: Singhal K, et al. Nature 620, 172–180 (2023). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06291-2

Erratum 004 — ESM3 生成式蛋白的授權與安全注意

Hayes 等 (2025) 釋出 ESM3 多模態蛋白生成模型，能依結構/功能 prompt 設計新序列。教學中若示範其生成功能，須提醒兩點：(1) ESM3 大型權重採 EvolutionaryScale Cambrian Open License，商業用途需另行授權；(2) 生成式蛋白存在 dual-use 風險，使用前應確認機構生物安全規範與 NTI / WHO 雙重用途研究指引。

Hayes et al. (2025) released ESM3, a multimodal protein generative model that designs novel sequences from structure / function prompts. If the tutorial demonstrates its generation pipeline, flag two issues: (1) the large ESM3 weights use the EvolutionaryScale Cambrian Open License, requiring separate licensing for commercial use; (2) generative proteins carry dual-use risk — confirm institutional biosafety policies and WHO / NTI dual-use research guidance before running prompts.

參考文獻：References: Hayes T, et al. Science 387:eads0018 (2025). DOI: 10.1126/science.ads0018

Erratum 005 — Model Card 不完整與 FUTURE-AI 落地建議

Mitchell 等 (2019) 提出 Model Card 概念，要求模型釋出附 intended use、限制、評估指標、人口偏差等欄位。然而在 HuggingFace 上至 2024 仍有 > 40% 生醫模型 card 缺乏 evaluation data 與 ethical considerations。Lekadir 等 (2025) FUTURE-AI 提供臨床落地的 30 項建議，涵蓋 Fairness / Universality / Traceability / Usability / Robustness / Explainability —— 教學釋出任何權重時，建議同步補完此六大面向。

Mitchell et al. (2019) introduced Model Cards, requiring intended use, limitations, evaluation metrics, demographic bias fields, etc. As of 2024 over 40% of biomedical models on HuggingFace still lack evaluation-data and ethical-considerations sections. Lekadir et al. (2025) FUTURE-AI offers 30 clinical-deployment recommendations across Fairness / Universality / Traceability / Usability / Robustness / Explainability — fill in all six dimensions when releasing weights from this tutorial.

參考文獻：References: Mitchell M, et al. FAT* '19, 220–229 (2019). DOI: 10.1145/3287560.3287596 · Lekadir K, et al. BMJ 388:e081554 (2025). DOI: 10.1136/bmj-2024-081554

Erratum 006 — TRIPOD+AI 2024 取代舊版報告規範

教學最後一章建議的 TRIPOD 2015 規範已被 Collins 等 (2024) 在 BMJ 發表的 TRIPOD+AI 取代。新版新增 27 項 AI 專屬條目，涵蓋演算法版本鎖定、訓練/外部驗證資料解耦、calibration plot、公平性子群分析、可重現性 code DOI 等。預測模型若涉及 AI/ML，應引用 TRIPOD+AI 而非 TRIPOD 2015。

The TRIPOD 2015 guideline recommended in the final chapter has been superseded by TRIPOD+AI (Collins et al. 2024, BMJ). The update adds 27 AI-specific items covering algorithm version pinning, separation of training / external-validation data, calibration plots, fairness subgroup analyses, and reproducibility code DOIs. Cite TRIPOD+AI rather than TRIPOD 2015 for any AI/ML prediction model.

參考文獻：References: Collins GS, Moons KGM, Dhiman P, et al. BMJ 385:e078378 (2024). DOI: 10.1136/bmj-2023-078378
