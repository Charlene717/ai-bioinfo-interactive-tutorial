AI 驅動藥物發現

標靶識別 → 分子生成 → 虛擬篩選 → ADMET → 臨床候選分子。AI 把「10 年 + 26 億美金」的傳統流程縮短了多少？又有哪些是仍未解決的硬限制？

Target ID → molecule generation → virtual screening → ADMET → clinical candidate. How much has AI shortened the canonical 10-year, $2.6B pipeline — and what hard limits remain?

總覽

一、AI 在藥物發現的五個關卡

標靶識別 (Target ID)

Open Targets、PandaOmics、CausalDB：整合 GWAS、scRNA-seq、文獻證據打分基因。

Open Targets, PandaOmics, CausalDB: integrate GWAS, scRNA-seq, and literature to score genes.

先導化合物 (Hit-to-Lead)

DiffDock + AlphaFold 3 做虛擬篩選；Pharmacophore-AI 找化學起點。

DiffDock + AlphaFold 3 for virtual screening; Pharmacophore-AI for chemical starts.

分子生成與最佳化

REINVENT 4、MolMIM、Pocket2Mol：條件生成滿足 docking + ADMET 多目標。

REINVENT 4, MolMIM, Pocket2Mol: conditional generation meeting docking + ADMET multi-objective constraints.

ADMET 預測

ADMET-AI、SwissADME、Ersilia models：CYP 抑制、hERG 心毒性、口服吸收等。

ADMET-AI, SwissADME, Ersilia models: CYP inhibition, hERG cardio toxicity, oral bioavailability.

臨床試驗預測

HINT、TrialGPT：預測 Phase I/II 成功率、患者匹配。

HINT, TrialGPT: predict Phase I/II success rate; patient matching.

生成式 AI

二、條件式分子生成的三種典範

🧪

SMILES LM

把 SMILES 當文字，用 GPT/RNN 生成。代表：REINVENT、Mol-CycleGAN。最簡單、容易控制屬性。

Treat SMILES as text, generate with GPT/RNN. Examples: REINVENT, Mol-CycleGAN. Simplest, easiest to constrain.

🎨

Graph diffusion

把分子當圖，用 diffusion 學原子 + 鍵的去噪。GraphDiff、DiGress。可保證價數合法。

Treat molecules as graphs, denoise atoms + bonds. GraphDiff, DiGress. Valence-correct by construction.

🧲

3D pocket-aware

給定蛋白口袋，直接在 3D 空間生成配體。Pocket2Mol、TargetDiff、PocketFlow。最有潛力但訓練資料有限。

Given a pocket, generate ligand atoms in 3D. Pocket2Mol, TargetDiff, PocketFlow. Most ambitious but data-limited.

動手

三、用 REINVENT 4 做多目標分子生成

# pip install reinvent
# reinvent_v4_config.toml — generate aspirin-like, low MW, hERG-safe
[parameters]
num_smiles = 10000
randomize_smiles = true

[scoring_function]
name = "custom_product"

[[scoring_components]]
component_type = "qed"             # Drug-likeness
weight = 1

[[scoring_components]]
component_type = "matching_substructure"
specific_parameters.smarts = ["c1ccccc1C(=O)O"]
weight = 1

[[scoring_components]]
component_type = "predictive_property"     # hERG model
specific_parameters.model_path = "hERG_classifier.pkl"
specific_parameters.transformation.type = "reverse_sigmoid"
weight = 1.5

# Run: reinvent reinvent_v4_config.toml
from admet_ai import ADMETModel

mdl = ADMETModel()
smiles = ["CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O",                       # aspirin
          "CC(=O)NC1=CC=C(O)C=C1",                        # acetaminophen
          "CC(C)Cc1ccc(C(C)C(=O)O)cc1"]                    # ibuprofen
df = mdl.predict(smiles=smiles)

# Returns 41 properties: solubility, hERG, CYP isoforms, BBB, oral_bioavailability...
print(df[["hERG_inhibition", "BBB_Martins", "Lipinski"]])

四、案例：INS018_055，第一個 AI-驅動的 Phase II 分子

2024 年 Insilico Medicine 將其用 AI 從新標靶 (TNIK) 發現的纖維化新藥 INS018_055 推進到 Phase II。從靶點識別到 Phase I 候選分子只用了 18 個月、260 萬美金——相較傳統 4-5 年、數千萬美金的標桿。

關鍵 AI 組件：(1) PandaOmics 從多組學資料識別 TNIK 為 IPF 標靶；(2) Chemistry42 生成 ~80,000 候選分子並排序；(3) 實驗合成 78 個分子，9 個在動物模型有效。

In 2024 Insilico Medicine advanced INS018_055, an AI-discovered fibrosis drug against the novel TNIK target, into Phase II. From target ID to Phase I candidate took 18 months and $2.6M — vs the traditional 4–5 years and tens of millions.

Key AI components: (1) PandaOmics identified TNIK as an IPF target from multi-omics; (2) Chemistry42 generated ~80,000 candidates with ranking; (3) 78 molecules synthesized, 9 active in animal models.

⚠️
但別過度樂觀：2026 年仍沒有「100% AI 設計、未經人工修改」的分子上市。AI 大幅加速「探索與排序」階段，但合成、安全性、臨床這些晚期環節依然是時間與失敗主因。 Don't get carried away: as of 2026 no "100% AI-designed, untouched-by-humans" molecule has been approved. AI accelerates exploration and ranking hugely, but synthesis, safety, and clinical phases remain dominant in cost and failure.
